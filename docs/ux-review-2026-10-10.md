# UX/UI review (10 Oct 2026)

Every tab and view checked in headless Chromium at 390px (plus desktop, dark mode and a first visit) against live data during GW6. The owner's priorities: bug fixes, design consistency and usability. Agreed order: bug fixes, then a small design system, then usability fixes built on it.

## Bugs

- FIXED: manager page said "Next: gameweek 6 v Daire. First meeting this season." while they were playing. The league feed shows this week's matches as 0-0 until the gameweek ends, so it now says "Playing now" with a Live score link.
- FIXED: League > Head to head said "They haven't played each other yet · next in GW6" during GW6. Same fix.

## Done (10 Oct)

- Design system: `docs/design-system.md`, `#/styleguide`.
- Live Fixtures and Bonus: by day in kick-off order, live open, finished folded, bonus Live / Waiting for FPL / Confirmed, double gameweek points per match.
- Header 270px to 168px (one-line deadlines, tap for all three). Section menus fade where more chips are hidden. Draft chips renamed (Steals & busts, Original squads, Point sources).
- Long explanations behind "How this works" on Draft, Odds, Suggestions, Trades, Trade network, Luck, Bonus.
- Trade cards fold to two lines (Trades ~10,700px to ~4,000px). Waiver battles fold by gameweek (~9,500px to ~3,600px). Waiver chips 32px tall.
- Plain labels: "in all" (was "on paper" and bare brackets), "void" (drop gone), "vs draft spot" (vs slot), "Claims won", "Lost out".
- Phone league table shows form (W/D/L columns move to desktop only); names stay on one line.

Still open: a proper player page, a "My team" view, the desktop layout, the old dense Live spacing values.

## Journeys

1. "How's my team?" has no front door. The header picker only highlights; your own page is reached by finding your name. Its squad list is plain text and shows 0 pts for players still to play.
2. Player pages hold only the ownership history: no form, fixtures, this week's points or news. "Search another player" goes to Moves > Journeys, which the header search now duplicates.
3. Trades (~10,700px on a phone) and Waivers (~9,500px) expand every card.
4. Suggestions and injury worries are the third chip under Moves; the deadline banner doesn't link to them.

## Usability

- Header (title, GW line, deadline banner, tabs) is ~270px, a third of a phone screen.
- League and Draft chip menus (6 each) cut off Records, Odds, Redraft and Draft day with no hint. Vague labels: Steals, No moves, Points.
- Explanation paragraphs before content (Bonus has two, Draft grades two stacked hints, Odds, Redraft). Better behind a "How this works" fold.
- Jargon: "3 (3)", "on paper", "drop gone", "vs slot".
- Phone league table hides form and +/-; "269 scored" wraps.
- Waiver manager chips are under 24px tall.

## Design consistency

- 16 font sizes (9px to 34px, including 10.5 and 11.5); much of it 10-12px.
- Green means mine, win, positive, active tab, link underline and "in 5 days".
- Players shown three ways (shirt rows on Live, plain text on manager page, table cells on Redraft). Six corner radii. "Draft grades" shows no grades.
- Desktop: narrow column; position chart text scales up very large.

## Plan

Design system, kept small: tokens (5-step type scale, spacing, 2-3 radii, colours named by meaning), about eight shared pieces (card, stat tile, chip/tag, player row, table, "How this works" fold, section header), a rulebook in `docs/design-system.md` that every session reads, and a hidden `#/styleguide` page. Then the usability fixes on top.
