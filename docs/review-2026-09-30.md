# Site review, 30 Sept 2026

A full review after step 5 (the Live tab). First a UX/UI walkthrough of every page at phone (390px) and desktop (1280px) sizes, in light and dark mode. Then regression testing of the data, the Worker API and the front end. Everything below was fixed in the same change unless it's marked **Not fixed**.

## How it was tested

- **Data:** `scripts/regression.ts` pulls the league's current data straight from FPL and runs every calculation the site does (25 checks). It covers:
  - the league table against FPL's own standings,
  - every H2H score in GW1-5 rebuilt two ways (from squads, and from live scoring with bonus and auto-subs),
  - trade values, waiver counts, player origins, squad origins adding up to points-for, pick values balancing to zero, the legal redraft, luck and records.

  All passed on live data. Run it again with `npx tsx scripts/regression.ts`.
- **API:** every production Worker route was checked for status, size, response time, cache headers, JSON shape and error messages. Also checked: unknown routes (404), writes (405) and deep links to the site (200). All fine.
- **Front end:** every page and League view was driven in a headless browser against real data, with no console errors. Four extra scenarios were tested with a local stand-in server:
  - a gameweek in progress,
  - finished,
  - pre-season (no gameweek yet),
  - one gameweek failing to load.
- **Unit tests:** 93, all passing before and after. The fixes were checked in the browser against the four scenarios above: first visit, choosing a team, just looking, filters, live, pre-season and a failing gameweek. The tab bar was checked at 360px and 390px. There were no JavaScript errors in any scenario.

## Bugs

| # | Severity | What | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | High | **Pre-season, the site never loads.** When FPL's current gameweek is 0 (next August, before GW1), the gameweek loader waits forever, so every page is stuck on "Loading the league…". | Gameweek 0 now means "no gameweeks yet". Pages show their empty states. |
| B2 | High | **One gameweek failing breaks the whole site.** If any `/api/gw/N` fails, every page shows "Couldn't load the league" and never recovers by itself, even pages that don't use gameweeks. | Gameweeks that fail are retried. The site loads with what it has and shows a note saying some gameweeks are missing. The error screen has a "Try again" button. |
| B3 | Medium | **"Updated X ago" is misleading.** It showed the oldest of all the data, including the player list (refreshed hourly) and draft picks (every 6 hours). So it often said "Updated 5 hours ago" when trades were 2 minutes old. | It now shows when the league, trades and waivers were last fetched. |
| B4 | Medium | **Header says "After gameweek 6" while GW6 is still being played.** | It now says "Gameweek 6 in progress" until FPL marks it finished. The Draft page wording follows the same rule. |
| B5 | Low | The Live tab fetched the same data twice when there was no next gameweek to show. | Only fetches the next gameweek when there is one. |
| B6 | Low | The error message was missing a full stop ("Couldn't load gameweek 3 Try again"). | Fixed, as part of B2's new error screen. |
| B7 | Low | Accessibility: the tabs didn't say which one is current, and the manager dropdowns had no labels for screen readers. | Added `aria-current` and labels. |

## UX and UI improvements

| # | What | Change |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | **The "Live" tab is cut off on phones.** Six tabs overflow by about 23px, so it looks like there are only five. | Tabs are tightened to fit a 360px phone. The Live tab gets a red dot while games are on. |
| U2 | **Everyone sees Adam's team as "mine".** The team picker was at the very bottom of long pages, so friends would never find it, and all the "you"/"Just mine" highlights pointed at Adam. | First visit shows a "Which team is yours?" prompt at the top. The picker also moves into the header, so it's always one tap away. |
| U3 | **Small tap targets.** Dropdowns were about 28px tall and checkboxes 13px. | Dropdowns are at least 36px. Filters are now buttons (see U4). |
| U4 | **Long lists only had "Just mine".** 20 trade cards and 61 waiver battles, with no way to look at one friend's moves. | The Trades and Waivers lists get a "Show" picker: everyone, just me, or any manager. |
| U5 | **Head to head opened on "you v Michael"** (alphabetical). | It now opens on your next opponent, or the last one if the season's over. |
| U6 | **Bare empty states** ("No trades to show."). | Friendlier messages, e.g. "You haven't made any trades yet." |
| U7 | **On desktop the purple header was a box floating in grey.** | The header band now runs the full width of the screen. |

## Checked and fine

- No page scrolls sideways at 390px. Tables and cards fit.
- Dark mode is readable on every page, and chart colours pass the colour-blind checks in both modes.
- Old `#/table` links still land on the League table, and unknown links go to Trades.
- Live page: provisional bonus, statuses and auto-subs display correctly mid-gameweek. Kick-off times show in the viewer's own time zone.
- The Worker refuses writes and never needs a login.

## Not fixed (ideas for later)

- **Pending trades sit at the top of the Trades list** as "Too early to call" cards. That's fine for now. It could be collapsed if the list gets long.
- **Trade card layout on small phones** is dense (two columns of players with notes). It's readable, but a single-column layout under 360px could help.
- **KV storage** is still off (see `docs/setup.md`), so a brand new Worker instance refetches finished gameweeks from FPL. That's harmless at this size.
